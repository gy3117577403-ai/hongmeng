# v1.34.140 — grouped platform navigation

The platform sidebar previously exposed a long flat list whose typography was affected by the active page. A shared navigation component now groups the existing entries into Production and Planning, Quality, Materials and Warehousing, Technology and Documents, People and Labor, and Collaboration and Approvals. Home and Reports remain direct entries; System Management and the planned Help entry sit in the footer.

Only one business group opens at a time. Parent rows expand without navigating; child rows keep the existing destinations and access filtering. A compact icon rail opens click-operated flyouts with viewport clamping, outside-click/focus dismissal, Escape and focus return. Expanded group preferences are stored per user, preserve an explicitly closed group on same-route reload, and resolve the owning group after a route change. The rail itself retains the existing initially compact behavior.

Navigation owns its font, row geometry, icons and light/orange state colors across workbenches. Desktop expanded width is 264px, compact width is 72px, and narrower screens use a 64px rail plus an overlay. Child targets are at least 44px high. Long menus scroll independently between the brand and fixed footer. Reduced-motion users receive no menu animation.

HR now mounts the same platform navigation and exposes a trigger beside its existing internal tabs. The employee directory's persistent Save/Cancel footer remains visible. Existing production/planning/warehouse business-mode controls and sample URLs remain available. No database schema or access-policy changes are included.
